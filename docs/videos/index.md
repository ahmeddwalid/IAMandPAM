# Video Series

Ten illustrated overviews, about seventy minutes in total, covering the same ground as the written reference. Each episode maps to a section of the site, and the link under the player opens the pages behind it.

They are orientation, not instruction. Watch one before you read a section to see the shape of the topic, or afterwards to check what stayed. The written pages remain the reference: they carry the sources, the error codes, and the detail these videos leave out. Where a video and a page disagree, the page is correct.

## How the episodes were made

Each episode was generated with NotebookLM from the pages in its section, then reviewed against them. The illustrations are generated sketch animation rather than screenshots, so they show the structure of an idea instead of the exact interface of a product. That makes them good for orientation and poor for procedure. For procedure, use the [labs](/docs/labs/) and the [code samples](/docs/code-samples/).

The last episode is in Arabic and covers the discipline as a whole. The rest are in English.

## Narrated walkthrough

A separate production, built from the site's own diagrams with burned-in subtitles. It covers the objects of identity, the AAA model, the lifecycle, least privilege, and where privileged access management fits.
