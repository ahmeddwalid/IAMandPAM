# Interview Preparation

This section is a question bank for identity and access management interviews, built the way the interview actually goes: conceptual questions first, protocol questions second, scenario and architecture questions third, behavioral questions last. Every answer has the same four-part structure:

- **Concise answer**: the 30-second version, which is what the interviewer expects first.
- **Detailed explanation**: the full reasoning, which is what separates a memorized answer from an understood one.
- **Why it matters**: the practical consequence, because interviewers hire for outcomes, not vocabulary.
- **Likely follow-ups**: the questions the answer invites, because the interviewer will probe what you just said.

The bank is built in waves. [Wave 1](01-conceptual-questions.md) covers the conceptual and protocol fundamentals. [Wave 2](03-scenario-questions.md) adds scenario and architecture questions that reuse the [scenario walkthroughs](/docs/scenarios/onboarding) and the [PAM architecture](/docs/pam-core/pam-architecture). [Wave 3](05-behavioral-questions.md) covers behavioral questions as templates you fill in with your own experience. The [cloud questions](06-cloud-questions.md) round out the bank for the cloud-heavy interviews that dominate the market.

## How to use this section

1. Read the question and answer it out loud before reading the treatment. The gap between your answer and the treatment is the study target.
2. Use the "likely follow-ups" as the next round: each follow-up is a question the interviewer may actually ask, so each treatment is the entry point to a cluster.
3. Cross-link out: the answers reference the site pages that go deeper. When an answer mentions the JIT access pattern, the linked page is what you read next.

The treatment is intentionally opinionated where the industry has a clear practice, such as the zero standing privilege rule. Interviewers respond better to a candidate with a position than a candidate who lists options.
