# Certifications & Career

The career section maps the field's credentials and progression, with the [content integrity rules](../interviews/index.md) applied strictly: every certification price was verified against official sources on 2026-08-18, and every salary figure is either sourced or explicitly labeled as a rough estimate with its basis stated.

## Pages in this section

1. [Certifications](/docs/careers/certifications): the six credentials this site's content maps to (Security+, SC-300, Okta Professional, SailPoint IdentityIQ Engineer, CyberArk Defender/Sentry, CISSP), with verified costs, prerequisites, difficulty, and the roles that ask for them, plus a sequencing recommendation.
2. [Career Ladder](/docs/careers/career-ladder): the IAM track (Analyst through Director) and the PAM track (Administrator through Architect), the skills that matter at each rung, and the honest note about PAM roles expecting prior experience.
3. [Middle East and Egypt](/docs/careers/middle-east-gulf-egypt): the Gulf and Egypt markets, what employers in each weight, and how the certifications and ladders apply locally.
4. [Resume and Portfolio Guide](/docs/careers/resume-portfolio): how to structure a resume for IAM roles, what hiring managers look for, portfolio project ideas, and GitHub presentation.
5. [Salary Negotiation](/docs/careers/salary-negotiation): how to research IAM salary ranges, negotiate compensation, evaluate offers, and navigate equity and benefits, with regional specifics.

## How this section fits the site

The [interview preparation](/docs/interviews/) section tells you how to answer questions; this section tells you which questions the market will ask. The certifications page grounds the career ladder in verifiable facts, the career ladder grounds the certifications in roles, and the regional page grounds both in the target market. The [hands-on labs](/docs/labs/) section is the delivery evidence that makes the whole combination credible in an interview.
