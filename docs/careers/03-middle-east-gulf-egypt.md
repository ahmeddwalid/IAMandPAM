# Middle East and Egypt

This page covers the site owner's target market directly. The global ladders on the [career ladder](/docs/careers/career-ladder) page apply, but the local market weights things differently: the Gulf runs fewer, larger identity programs on a compressed ladder, and Egypt runs a consulting and outsourcing market where certified depth travels well. Salary figures here are **rough estimates with their basis stated**, not survey data; Gulf salaries vary widely with employer type (government, bank, multinational, consulting), and Egypt salaries vary with employer type and currency movement.

## The Gulf market (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait)

**Demand drivers.** Government digital transformation programs (the UAE's national digital strategies, Saudi Vision 2030's technology pillars) and the banking sector's regulatory posture drive real identity programs: the region's banks and government entities run enterprise IAM, IGA, and PAM estates, and the hiring follows. Cybersecurity is a stated national priority in the UAE and Saudi Arabia, which keeps identity hiring steady relative to the wider tech market.

**What employers weight.**

- **Government and banking roles weight certification plus nationality-related compliance contexts**, and the enterprise vendors matter: SailPoint and CyberArk appear heavily in the installed base, with Microsoft Entra ID as the directory layer. The [certification map](/docs/careers/certifications) priorities hold: SC-300 for Microsoft-centric roles, CyberArk Defender/Sentry for PAM roles.
- **The ladder is compressed.** Titles like "Senior IAM Engineer" and "IAM Architect" are attached to less tenure than the US ladder above implies; a strong 3-5 year candidate can hold an architect title. The interview bar is practical: scenario and architecture questions of exactly the type in the [interview section](/docs/interviews/) dominate.
- **Remote and relocation patterns.** Multinationals and consultancies hire from Egypt and elsewhere into Gulf roles, which makes the Gulf a downstream target for the Egypt market below. The interview process for these roles is often entirely remote until the final stage.

**Estimate (Gulf, basis: observed posting ranges for IAM/PAM roles across banking, government, and consulting in UAE and Saudi Arabia; wide variance by employer type)**: identity engineers roughly AED 18k-28k monthly in the UAE and SAR 20k-35k monthly in Saudi Arabia, with architect-level roles above that range at government-adjacent employers. Treat the range as directional, not a quote.

## The Egypt market

**Demand drivers.** Egypt's security market is anchored by two engines: the local banking sector (the Central Bank of Egypt's cybersecurity framework drives identity and PAM investment) and a large outsourcing and consulting industry that delivers identity work for Gulf, European, and US clients. For a candidate, the practical consequence: identity skills here are exportable, and the jobs that pay best are often the ones working on foreign programs from Cairo or remotely.

**What employers weight.**

- **Certification plus delivery evidence.** The outsourcing market sells certified capacity, so credentials (SC-300, Okta Professional, CyberArk, and CISSP later) translate directly into billable roles. The [hands-on labs](/docs/labs/) section matters more in this market than anywhere else, because delivery companies verify claims in interviews and take-home exercises.
- **English plus delivery language.** Identity documentation and vendor consoles are English-first, and the outsourcing delivery chain runs in English; this site's English-first design matches the market.
- **The PAM niche is real.** Egyptian security teams are a known source of PAM and IGA delivery talent for Gulf banks, which is the strongest argument for the PAM specialization on this site: it is the highest-value identity skill to sell from Egypt.

**Estimate (Egypt, basis: observed ranges across Egyptian banks, outsourcing firms, and remote international contracts; currency-sensitive)**: identity roles typically EGP 40k-90k monthly at local employers, with the upper half of the range for PAM specialization and international-contract work; international remote contracts can exceed local ranges by multiples. These are directional planning numbers, not quotes.

## What the two markets share

- **The enterprise vendor stack dominates.** Microsoft Entra ID as the directory, SailPoint for IGA, CyberArk for PAM: the [vendor landscape](/docs/vendors/major-vendors) section is the direct map of the installed base.
- **CISSP is the senior currency.** Architect and management roles in both markets list it as preferred or required, with the same [eligibility rules](/docs/careers/certifications) as everywhere else.
- **Interview style is practical.** Both markets lean on scenario and architecture questions, and the [Wave 2 bank](/docs/interviews/scenario-questions) was built with exactly that style in mind.
